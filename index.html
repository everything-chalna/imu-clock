Set the real time on the flight controller before recording

How to use (takes 1 minute, do it every time you power on):
  1. Power on the flight controller (battery or USB).
  2. Close the SpeedyBee app completely (swipe it away). Only one app can be connected over Bluetooth at a time.
  3. Open this page in Google Chrome (Android phone or laptop). Turn on Bluetooth (and Location on Android).
  4. Press the big blue button, pick the SpeedyBee device in the list, press Pair/Connect.
  5. Wait for the green "Done" box. The page disconnects by itself.
  6. Now open the SpeedyBee app and record as usual. Do not power off in between — the clock is lost at power-off.
If anything goes wrong, press "Copy diagnostic log" and paste it to us. iPhone/iPad: Safari/Chrome can't do Bluetooth — open this link in the free Bluefy app instead.
